# Visual guide

- `service_volume_vs_sla_risk.png` — request volume against the share breached or at risk; bubble size represents average handoffs.
- `resolution_time_tail.png` — resolution-time distributions by service type on a log scale.
- `handoff_delay_curve.png` — median resolution time by number of handoffs.
- `inflow_vs_clearance.png` — arrivals and completed work by month, alongside reconstructed month-end backlog.
- `team_mix_adjusted_risk.png` — observed risk compared with a simple expectation based on priority and service type.
- `sla_risk_heatmap.png` — the share breached or at risk by service type and priority.
- `queue_attention_score.png` — distribution of the rule-based request attention score.

All figures use the generated sample. Percentages and hours are illustrative, not service targets or company performance claims.
